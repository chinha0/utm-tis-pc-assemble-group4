## 💭 What We Learned

- Identify the CPU, RAM, motherboard, power supply unit (PSU), storage devices, and CPU cooler.
- Understand the purpose of each component and where it is installed.
- Recognise common power and data connections, including the 24-pin motherboard cable, CPU power cable, SATA data cable, and SATA power connector.
- Follow a careful assembly sequence and observe electrostatic-discharge (ESD) precautions.
- Distinguish a **hard disk drive (HDD)** from a **CD/DVD optical drive**. They are different devices, even though both may be installed inside a desktop PC.

---

## 🦺 Important Safety Notes

- Switch off and unplug the PC before installing or removing components.
- Avoid touching gold contacts, CPU socket pins, and circuit-board contacts.
- Discharge static electricity by using an ESD wrist strap or touching an unpainted metal part of the case before handling components.
- Never force a component into a slot or connector. Check its alignment and the motherboard manual.
- Never open the PSU casing. It can contain hazardous electrical components even when unplugged.
- Use the motherboard and component manuals for model-specific details. The photos in this repository do not establish the exact model or specification of every part.

## 📖 Learning Outcome

This practical helped us connect theoretical computer-hardware knowledge with the physical components inside a desktop computer. It also improved our awareness of safe handling, component identification, cable connections, and the logical sequence used when assembling a PC.
